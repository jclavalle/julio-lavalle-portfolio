## When the route changes, cash runs short before margin shows it

DSPs are paid per stop, but drivers, fuel and suppliers are paid on their own calendar. When the platform changes route density or duration, the operator feels it twice: in weekly margin, and in the days between paying out and getting paid.

In the modeled case, the platform moves routes from 120 stops in 8 hours to 90 stops in 10 hours at the same R$4 per stop. Weekly contribution falls 75%, and the cash balance goes negative on November 11, before the next collection arrives.

## Show margin and cash timing in the same view

I put weekly contribution beside a dated, day-by-day cash forecast so the operator sees both questions at once: is the route still worth it, and will I make payroll and fuel? Pay stays at R$4 per stop in both plans, so the comparison isolates route density and duration.

## An advisor that compares options, not one that repeats the dashboard

In my first walkthrough, the AI advisor mostly read the dashboard back. I reworked it so it gets calculated alternatives from the model, asks whether an option is feasible, and only then proposes a plan. If the operator accepts, the plan is applied to the simulation and the server recalculates before explaining the result.

## Walk into the platform conversation prepared

The last step is the conversation that actually fixes the problem. The advisor turns the scenario into a negotiation brief: which route change to ask for, what it does to cash, and the specific question on density or duration. The brief can be copied, downloaded or exported as a scenario file.
