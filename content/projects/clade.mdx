## Understand what trial teams actually experienced

Enterprise trials typically lasted around two weeks, making it important to see where users were progressing and where they were getting stuck. I created recurring monitoring routines and a Trial Watch dashboard that combined activity data, qualitative observations and LogRocket session and error evidence.

The goal was to connect what a client was trying to achieve with the behavior and issues visible in the product.

## Turn observations into engineering follow-through

Visibility mattered when it led to action. I automated the creation of engineering stories from trial findings and routed issues to engineers so the team could investigate and ship fixes quickly. I coordinated end-of-day cherry-picks for ready fixes.

## Expand regression coverage at a lower cost

Alongside the trial-feedback loop, I built an internal end-to-end testing approach to check important product journeys repeatedly. Playwright tests and nightly runs provided a complementary signal to customer observations, while AI-assisted triage supported failure investigation and proposed repairs.
